WL@UW

Wheeled Lab @ University of Washington

WL@UW is a space between traditional robotics clubs at UW and academic robotics research at CSE. It is designed for students who are just beginning (or thinking about beginning) robotics research in data-driven methods like Reinforcement Learning, Robot Simulation, Computer Vision, etc.

Wheeled Lab students are motivated by their own research questions and are supported through the Robot Learning Laboratory to enable hands-on development (project scoping, robot hardware, compute, design space, etc.).
